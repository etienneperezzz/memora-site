The 8 import steps

1-3 / 8

  1. Extraction without overwriting

    Your .zip files are extracted. A new export only adds what's new.

    Details

    Snapchat splits big exports into several files: Memorizzz handles them all in one place. Your original export stays intact.

    .zipincremental import

  2. The real date

    Snapchat's UTC time becomes the local time of where the shot was taken.

    Details

    Dates are stored separately, in memories_history.json. The time zone is found offline via GPS: a snap taken at 11pm in Tokyo is dated 11pm, not 2pm.

    memories_history.jsontime zone via GPS

  3. Captions merged back

    Your text and stickers, delivered separately, find their photo again.

    Details

    The PNG layer is merged pixel-perfect onto photos, and burned frame by frame into videos, re-encoded to H.264.

    PillowffmpegH.264

  4. Embedded metadata

    Date and GPS position are written into every file.

    Details

    EXIF for photos, MP4 atoms for videos: your memories stay properly dated in Apple Photos or Google Photos, even without Memorizzz.

    EXIFGPSMP4 ©day

  5. Readable layout

    Files renamed by date and filed by month.

    Details

    Each file is timestamped to when it was taken: within a day, the order matches when you took them.

    AAAA-MM-JJ_…AAAA/MM

  6. Videos stitched

    Chat videos split into 10 s pieces are reassembled.

    Details

    Memorizzz reads each piece's creation time, groups consecutive ones and stitches them without re-encoding. Voice notes are detected and shown as audio.

    creation_timelossless concat

  7. Map and stats

    Places resolved offline, thumbnails kept in cache.

    Details

    The app's built-in geocoding powers the map and the stats. The gallery stays smooth even with tens of thousands of items.

    offline geocodingcache

  8. Conversations linked

    Every message gets its photo, video or voice note back.

    Details

    The chat history is linked to the export's media: each conversation shows up just like in Snapchat, with its stats. Nothing is sent online.

    chat_history.jsonSQLite

Frequently asked questions

Saving your Snapchat memories, made simple.

How do I save all my Snapchat memories at once?

Request your “My Data” export from Snapchat in JSON format, then drop the file(s) you receive into Memorizzz. The app processes your whole export at once and arranges it into a gallery sorted by day, on your computer.

Can I download my Snapchat memories for free?

Yes. Memorizzz processes your full export and lets you freely browse your 100 most recent memories. After that, it's a one-time 4,99 € for 1,000 memories, 1,000 Chat Media and 1,000 messages, or 19,99 € for your whole export, no subscription. It's a local “Snapchat memories saver”, with no watermark or hidden download limit.

Will Snapchat delete my memories in 2027?

No, it archives them. Starting in January 2027, memories older than a year that go beyond your first 5 GB stay on Snapchat as thumbnails, but you need a storage plan to open, edit or share them. Memories from the last year and your oldest 5 GB stay accessible. With your export and Memorizzz, you keep everything, in full, with no subscription.

Which plan should I choose?

Essential at 4,99 € is enough to keep your 1,000 most recent memories, Chat Media and messages. Unlimited at 19,99 € unlocks your whole export, even if it holds tens of thousands. Both are one-time, lifetime purchases, chosen in the app.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. All processing happens on your computer. Unlike online download tools, your Snapchat export, your photos and your videos are never sent to any server.

Do I need a Snapchat+ subscription?

No. Once your memories are saved with Memorizzz, you have a full copy for life, without depending on Snapchat or a monthly subscription.

Which systems does Memorizzz run on?

Memorizzz runs on macOS 11+ (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11, and Linux x64.

Is there an iPhone or Android app?

Coming soon. An iPhone app, then Android, is in the works, with the same interface and the same features. Until then, Memorizzz runs on your computer, and you can keep your memories on an external hard drive.
