The 7 import steps

1-3 / 7

  1. Extraction without overwriting

    Your .zip files are extracted. A new export only adds what's new.

    Details

    Snapchat splits big exports into several files: Memora handles them all in one place. Your original export stays intact.

    .zipincremental import

  2. The real date

    Snapchat's UTC time becomes the local time of where the shot was taken.

    Details

    Dates are stored separately, in memories_history.json. The time zone is found offline via GPS: a snap taken at 11pm in Tokyo is dated 11pm, not 2pm.

    memories_history.jsontime zone via GPS

  3. Captions merged back

    Your text and stickers, delivered separately, find their photo again.

    Details

    The PNG layer is merged pixel-perfect onto photos, and burned frame by frame into videos, re-encoded to H.264.

    PillowffmpegH.264

  4. Embedded metadata

    Date and GPS position are written into every file.

    Details

    EXIF for photos, MP4 atoms for videos: your memories stay properly dated in Apple Photos or Google Photos, even without Memora.

    EXIFGPSMP4 ©day

  5. Readable layout

    Files renamed by date and filed by month.

    Details

    Each file is timestamped to when it was taken: within a day, the order matches when you took them.

    AAAA-MM-JJ_…AAAA/MM

  6. Videos stitched

    Chat videos split into 10 s pieces are reassembled.

    Details

    Memora reads each piece's creation time, groups consecutive ones and stitches them without re-encoding. Voice notes are detected and shown as audio.

    creation_timelossless concat

  7. Map and stats

    Places resolved offline, thumbnails kept in cache.

    Details

    The app's built-in geocoding powers the map and the Rétro. The gallery stays smooth even with tens of thousands of items.

    offline geocodingcache

Frequently asked questions

Saving your Snapchat memories, made simple.

How do I save all my Snapchat memories at once?

Request your “My Data” export from Snapchat in JSON format, then drop the file(s) you receive into Memora. The app processes your whole export at once and arranges it into a gallery sorted by day, on your computer.

Can I download my Snapchat memories for free?

Yes. Memora processes your full export and lets you freely browse your 100 most recent memories. To unlock everything, it's a one-time 19,99 €, no subscription. It's a local “Snapchat memories saver”, with no watermark or hidden download limit.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. All processing happens on your computer. Unlike online download tools, your Snapchat export, your photos and your videos are never sent to any server.

Do I need a Snapchat+ subscription?

No. Once your memories are saved with Memora, you have a full copy for life, without depending on Snapchat or a monthly subscription.

Which systems does Memora run on?

Memora runs on macOS 11+ (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11, and Linux x64.
